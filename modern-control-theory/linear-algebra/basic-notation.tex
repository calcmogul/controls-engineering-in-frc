\section{Basic notation}

The dimensionality of a matrix is specified by a row-column pair. For example, a
matrix with two rows and three columns is a two-by-three matrix. Matrices are
named with uppercase letters while vectors are named with lowercase letters. An
optional subscript ${m \times n}$ denotes the matrix having $m$ rows and $n$
columns.
\begin{equation*}
  \mat{x} =
  \begin{bmatrix}
    1 \\
    2
  \end{bmatrix}
  \quad
  \mat{A}_{2 \times 3} =
  \begin{bmatrix}
    1 & 2 & 3 \\
    4 & 5 & 6
  \end{bmatrix}
\end{equation*}
